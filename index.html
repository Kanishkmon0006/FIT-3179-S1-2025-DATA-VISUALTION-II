C & D. Socio-economic (IRSAD) and Migration

Socio-economic advantage (IRSAD) relates to migrant mix. The top chart shows SA2 deciles; the bottom shows SA3 means with a trend.

Takeaways

  • Overseas-born share spans all deciles, skewed higher in advantaged areas.
  • SA3-level mean IRSAD vs overseas-born shows a mild negative slope, suggesting highly diverse pockets in mid-to-higher advantage zones.
